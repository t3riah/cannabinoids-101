LEVEL 4

CYP450 Drug Checker & Binding Safety

💊 CYP450 Drug Interaction Checker Enzyme Competition

⚠️ Warfarin & Blood Thinners High Interaction Risk

CBD competitively inhibits CYP2C9 enzymes responsible for breaking down Warfarin. This slows clearance, leading to elevated blood concentrations and increased internal bleeding risk.

⚖️ Plant Agonists vs. Synthetic Full Agonists

Plant Partial Agonists (THC): Natural plant-derived THC acts as a partial agonist at CB1. It has built-in signaling ceilings that limit maximal receptor activation, preventing fatal overdose.
Synthetic Full Agonists ("Spice" / K2): Lab-created synthetic cannabinoids bind CB1 as full agonists with 100x potency. They rapidly desensitize receptors, triggering severe cardiac arrhythmia, seizures, and toxic psychosis.
